ARC: mystery_twist (question → picture → tension → mechanism → twist → fix → evidence → answer). This is the arc of the platform's reference short.
Beats in order; one beat is one scene. Every scene ends by leaving the question that the next scene's first line picks up.
1 hook (section hook, 2–3 lines): a contrast the viewer can feel (hundreds of steps vs one, size, speed, cost) plus one open question the paper answers. Build it from a tension fact when one exists.
2 picture (section background): the single object the viewer must picture to follow everything else, defined in the header and drawn from a setup fact when one exists. Later scenes reuse this frame.
3 tension (section problem or method): why a careful person would doubt the approach, or what nobody knew. Exactly one everyday metaphor, mapped back to the paper's object in the same scene.
4 mechanism (section theory or method): the main result shown as something that moves, as a parameter sweep on the beat-2 frame, with its setting or assumption stated on screen.
5 twist (section problem or limitation): where beat 4 stops holding in practice (a counterexample, a failure regime), shown concretely. Skip this beat if no fact supports it; never invent one.
6 fix (section method): the paper's remedy, shown by turning one knob on a picture, naming the threshold or range the paper gives.
7 evidence (section result): one chart from the paper's main table; every number with dataset, metric and condition; at most one derived comparison with its inputs.
8 answer (section takeaway, then outro, 2–3 lines): answer the hook's question with one line per claim, then the arXiv card.
