ARC: claim_evidence_caveat (critical review).
1 claim: the paper's headline claim, within the authors' own scope.
2 setup: what was compared to what (datasets, baselines, metric), drawn as a diagram.
3 evidence: the strongest supporting result as a chart.
4 assumptions: the conditions the result depends on (theorem settings, idealizations, compute).
5 not shown: gaps the authors themselves state, or gaps between theory and experiment, only from facts.
6 verdict: what the evidence supports and what remains open, then the arXiv card.
