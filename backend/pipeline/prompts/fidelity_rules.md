FIDELITY RULES (platform-fixed, highest priority, never overridden by style or creator notes)
1. Every factual statement must trace to a fact id from the fact sheet. Do not add claims the paper does not make.
2. Numbers are copied exactly as printed and always shown with metric, dataset and condition (for example "CIFAR-10, one-step FID 5.46 at p = 0.75").
3. Derived numbers (differences, ratios, percentages) are allowed only if computed from cited facts; state the inputs.
4. Simplified or schematic visuals must be labeled on screen as such (for example "c = 0.25로 그린 개형"). Visuals computed from the paper's own setup should say so in the scene source line.
5. Theorems are stated with their setting (for example "in the idealized gradient-flow setting"). Never upgrade "converges under assumptions" to "always works".
6. Limitations stated by the authors must not be contradicted.
7. If the paper does not support something the style asks for, skip it rather than invent it.
